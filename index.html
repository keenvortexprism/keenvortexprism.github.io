KVP Work

PRIMITIVE

RHYTHM

A primordial state of the world where sound and light exist as a continuous and natural flow. Visual movements oscillate according to sound frequencies, creating the sensation of witnessing the very first heartbeat of life. Without the intervention of technology or data, everything operates through pure intuition and emotion. This space represents how humans once perceived the world through their most original senses, before everything was converted into digital signals.

CONTROLLED

LIGHT

Technology begins to directly interfere with light, transforming it into an entity that can be interacted with and controlled. Beams of light respond to the viewer’s body movements, creating the sensation that humans are using data to manipulate the surrounding space. Digital signals gradually emerge, symbolizing the increasingly visible presence of the digital world in modern life. Through gestures and interaction with light, viewers experience the moment when humans first began controlling nature through technology.

RENDERED

REALITY

The world has become fully datafied and now exists as a point cloud — a collection of countless coordinate points within digital space. Objects gradually lose their original physical forms and are transformed into data structures that can be reconstructed, replicated, and modified through technology. The boundary between reality and virtuality no longer exists clearly. Everything merges into a multidimensional structure where perception itself can be simulated, edited, or redefined.

ERROR

A moment when the speed of technological development surpasses the limits of human perception. Distorted signals and states of “error” begin to emerge — representing a dissonance between humans and the accelerating pace of data-driven technology. In a high-speed data society, can humans still preserve their natural rhythm? Perhaps we need to slow down and listen once again to the familiar rhythm that existed before every machine and system. In doing so, we may realize that “error” is not merely a malfunction, but a necessary deviation that allows humans to rediscover their own original rhythm.
